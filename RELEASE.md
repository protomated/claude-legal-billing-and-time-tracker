# Legal Billing and Time Tracker v1.0.0

Initial release.

## What's included

### `legal-billing.mcpb` — Legal Billing and Time Tracker

Log billable hours, generate invoices, manage trust account deposits and
withdrawals, and monitor revenue — by chatting naturally in Claude Desktop.
Your data lives in your own Google Sheet. No monthly subscription, no relay,
no shared credentials.

**Actions:**
- **Log time** — "Log 2 hours for John Smith on Smith v. Jones at $350"
- **Generate invoices** — "Invoice John Smith for all unbilled work"
- **Record payments** — "John Smith paid his invoice"
- **Trust deposits** — "Add a $5,000 retainer for Mary Lee"
- **Trust withdrawals** — "Withdraw $500 from Sarah's trust for a filing fee"
- **Revenue dashboard** — "Show me my billing dashboard"

## Setup

Install time: ~3 minutes.

1. Copy the Legal Billing template to your Google Drive (File → Make a copy)
2. Install `legal-billing.mcpb` in Claude Desktop → Extensions → Add
3. When prompted, paste your sheet URL
4. In a new chat, say "connect Google" — a browser tab opens for one-time sign-in

No relay. No service account. No shared credentials.

## Compliance

Trust/IOLTA entries follow standard three-way reconciliation structure
(bookkeeping — not legal advice). Review all trust entries against your state
bar's trust-accounting guidance before relying on them. All billing outputs
carry a review disclaimer. There is no undo — errors are corrected directly
in the Google Sheet.
